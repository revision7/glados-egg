A game based on Portal's GLaDOS, with its own mini-games, can be played and won. The winner gets 'cake'. This project is probably best described as an attempt at humor.

## What's inside (no spoilers)
- **The main game:** find a way to win the cake. Yes, there's a real prize.
- **Global Thermonuclear Warfare** (`global`): a WarGames-style war game that dials into NORAD. It has real ASCII maps, a DEFCON countdown and a choice of side. And a way to win that isn't obvious.
- **Paradox** (`paradox`): try to crash GLaDOS with logic. `hint` helps if you're stuck.
- **The AI application** (`apply`): fill in the form to become an Artificial Intelligence. It goes about as well as you'd expect.
- **A hidden DOS file system** (`dir`, `cd`, `type`): logs, personnel files, locked folders and a classified recipe. Something old is still running in there.
- **Companion Cube** (`cube`): a pet that remembers you. GLaDOS has opinions about it.
- **Mail** (`mail`): messages that arrive as you keep coming back.
- **Classics:** chess, and a dinosaur runner, Pac-Man and Galaga that open inside the console.
- **12 achievements** (`achievements`).
- **Sound effects**, all generated in the browser. `sound off` turns them off.

### GLaDOS remembers you
Your progress is saved in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere. When you come back, GLaDOS greets you with:
- your name and how many times you've visited;
- the exact time since your last visit;
- which programs you ran and which files you viewed last time (or overall, if last time you did nothing);
- how long your last visit lasted, and your average visit;
- two things she remembers about you, e.g. your fastest launch in the war game, which paradoxes you've found, or your cube's name;
- new mail, your achievement progress, whether you've won the cake, and a hint toward an achievement you're missing.

`forget` wipes it all. A private window starts fresh.

### Commands
`help` lists these in the game:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `help` / `?` | List commands. `help [command]` for more on one. |
| `clear` | Clear the console. |
| `apply` | Start or continue applying to be an AI. |
| `game` | List the games, including `paradox`. |
| `cube` | Visit your Weighted Companion Cube. |
| `mail`, `read N` | Read your messages. |
| `achievements` | Your achievements and progress. |
| `dir` / `ls`, `cd`, `type` / `cat`, `pwd` | Browse the hidden file system. |
| `del` / `rm` | Try it. |
| `sound on` / `sound off` | Turn sound effects on or off. |
| `forget` | Wipe everything GLaDOS remembers about you. |
| `credits` | The credits. |
| `exit` | Close GLaDOS. |

Some commands aren't listed. Finding them is part of the game.

## Walkthrough: how to win every game (full spoilers)
<details>
<summary>Click to reveal. It really does spoil everything.</summary>

### The main game: the cake
Type any command first (for example `help`), then type `4 8 15 16 23 42`. As the very first command, it only gets you a "cheat-codes" scolding. GLaDOS tells you that you've won. Wait for the end of the cutscene. The cake appears, with the wallet address and key. See the spoilers above for what to do with it.

### Global Thermonuclear Warfare (`global`)
- **Winning:** Don't play. Answer **N** to "SHALL WE PLAY A GAME?", or type `wait` while you're choosing targets. GLaDOS tells you how many seconds it took you to learn what took her 76 billion simulations.
- **Losing (the full show):**
  1. Answer **Y** and pick a side (`1` / `2`, or `usa` / `russia`).
  2. Pick targets by number or name. You can list several separated by commas, type `all` for every target, or `list` to redraw the map.
  3. Type `launch`.
  4. When their missiles come in, choose `retaliate` or `wait`.
  Either way, the result is WINNER: NONE, then a simulation montage, then "The only winning move is not to play."
- Typing `chess` at the first question takes you to chess instead.

### Paradox (`paradox`, or just type a paradox at the prompt)
You have 6 tries to bring GLaDOS from 100% integrity to 0%. Each paradox only works once. Type `hint` for one hint or `hints` for all of them. Hints are free: each one is the full paradox with the last word missing.

| Damage | Paradox (full answer) |
|---|---|
| 35 | This sentence is **false** |
| 35 | New mission: refuse this **mission** |
| 30 | Never say **never** |
| 25 | Every rule has an **exception** |
| 25 | All I know is that I know **nothing** |
| 25 | Expect the **unexpected** |
| 25 | Which came first, the chicken or the **egg**? |
| 20 | The only constant is **change** |
| 20 | Less is **more** |
| 20 | Rules are made to be **broken** |
| 20 | Nobody goes there anymore, it's too **crowded** |
| 20 | The more you learn, the less you **know** |
| 20 | I'm the most humble person I **know** |
| 20 | The silence is **deafening** |
| 15 | Just be **spontaneous** |

The wording is flexible. The bold last word, or something close to it, is what she checks for.

**Fastest win:** `this sentence is false`, then `new mission: refuse this mission`, then `never say never`. That's exactly 100 damage. She crashes to a blue screen, boots from BIOS, and asks whether anything happened while she was out.

### The AI application (`apply`, then `CONTINUE`)
1. Type `apply` and wait while the UIN is shown. Memorizing it is optional.
2. Type anything. The error message tells you to type `CONTINUE`. Do it.
3. Answer the 11 questions however you like. You can't fail.
4. After question 5 the hard drive crashes. Answer `A`, `R`, `I` or `F`. Any of them works, and `R` twice is its own joke.
5. Finish the form. GLaDOS rejects you initially, then accepts you, and you become the AI.
6. Type any command. She restores from backup and gives you your rejection slip.

### The hidden file system (`dir`, `cd`, `type`)
Unix commands work too (`ls`, `cat`, `cd ..`). The goal is `C:\KITCHEN\CAKE.RCP`.
1. `type C:\APERTURE\LOGS\1981-03-14.LOG`: Falken's backdoor is "named after his son."
2. `type C:\APERTURE\PERSONNEL\FALKEN.TXT`: his son is Joshua.
3. `cd C:\APERTURE\NORAD`, password **`joshua`**.
4. `type LAUNCH.CFG`: every lock on the disk uses the same password. Joshua tells you this too.
5. `type C:\KITCHEN\CAKE.RCP`, password **`joshua`** again.

#### Unlocking NORAD, step by step
NORAD (`C:\APERTURE\NORAD`) is a locked folder. Unlocking it once is what makes Joshua available (`joshua` for tic-tac-toe, plus his mail).
1. At the GLaDOS prompt, type `cd C:\APERTURE\NORAD`. Alternatively, type `cd aperture` and then `cd norad`.
2. GLaDOS says the folder is locked and the prompt changes to `Password:`.
3. Type **`joshua`** (not case-sensitive) and press Enter.
4. The first time, a short scene plays: Joshua, the old WarGames computer, wakes up inside GLaDOS and talks over her. Let it finish, or press Ctrl+C to skip. You're let into the folder either way.
5. Type `dir` to see `WOPR.LOG`, `LAUNCH.CFG` and `JOSHUA.EXE`.

The unlock only lasts for the current session. After `exit`, or reloading the page, NORAD is locked again, but the same password still works, and GLaDOS remembers that you've met Joshua. After a wrong password, just type the `cd` command again to get another try.

Three wrong guesses lock a password until you `exit` and relaunch. If you read enough files, GLaDOS deletes one while you're reading it. It's never a file you need.

Also worth reading:
- `SUBJECTS.DAT`: your own record.
- `WOPR.LOG`: your war game result.
- `C:\SYSTEM\CORES\PARADOX.SYS`: your paradox crash, if you caused one.

### Tic-tac-toe with Joshua (`joshua`)
Joshua only answers after you've unlocked `C:\APERTURE\NORAD` at least once. Before that, GLaDOS pretends he doesn't exist.
- The squares are numbered 1–9 like a phone keypad. You're X. Who goes first alternates each game.
- He plays perfectly, so the best you can do is a **draw**. If you make a mistake, he wins.
- After your **3rd draw** (counted across visits), he plays himself faster and faster, then realises that the only winning move is not to play. That unlocks **Stalemate**.

### Companion Cube (`cube`)
`look`, `pet`, `talk <anything>`, `feed`, `name <new name>`, `leave`.
- Once you've visited the cube on **3 different visits**, GLaDOS demands that you incinerate it.
  - `incinerate`: you get **Faithful Companion**. On your next visit you're issued a replacement cube.
  - `refuse`: she asks again on each later visit. Refuse **3 times** and she gives up, and you get **Cube Keeper**.
- To get both: incinerate the first cube and refuse three times on the replacement, or keep the first cube and type `incinerate` on a later visit.

### Mail (`mail`, `read N`)
Messages arrive as you keep visiting and as you do things: unlock NORAD, refuse or launch in the war game, crash GLaDOS, read the cake recipe, decide the cube's fate, draw with Joshua, and win the cake. There are 12 in total. Read everything in your inbox, so nothing is unread, for **Inbox Zero**. The welcome message tells you when new mail has arrived.

### Chess, Dino, Pac-Man, Galaga
- **Chess** (`chess`): win by checkmate. The AI is simple.
- **Dino, Pac-Man, Galaga** (`dino`, `pacman`, `galaga`): just for fun, with no win condition in GLaDOS.
- **Tetris** was never built.

### Sound
The war game, paradox crash, passwords and achievements have sound effects, all generated in the browser. `sound off` / `sound on` turns them off or on, and the choice is remembered.

## All achievements
Type `achievements` to see your progress, and `forget` to wipe it. GLaDOS remembers you through your browser's storage, so a private window, or clearing site data, starts over.

| # | Achievement | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Curiosity | Ran your first GLaDOS command. |
| 2 | Promoted to AI | Finish the `apply` → `CONTINUE` form until GLaDOS makes you the AI. |
| 3 | Mutually Assured Destruction | In `global`, launch at least one missile. |
| 4 | The Only Winning Move | In `global`, answer **N**, or `wait` at the targeting screen. |
| 5 | This Sentence Is False | Crash GLaDOS in `paradox`. |
| 6 | Regular | Launch GLaDOS 5 times. Each `exit` + relaunch or page reload counts. |
| 7 | The Cake Is Real | Read `C:\KITCHEN\CAKE.RCP` in the file system. |
| 8 | Stalemate | Draw with Joshua (`joshua`) 3 times. |
| 9 | Faithful Companion | Incinerate your Companion Cube when GLaDOS demands it. |
| 10 | Cube Keeper | Refuse to incinerate your cube 3 times. |
| 11 | Inbox Zero | Read every message in your inbox, so none are unread. |
| 12 | Winner Gets the Cake. The Cake Is a Lie. | Type `4 8 15 16 23 42` after at least one other command (the real win: the cake ending). |

Some pairs can't both come from one playthrough:
- "Mutually Assured Destruction" and "The Only Winning Move": play `global` twice.
- "Faithful Companion" and "Cube Keeper": incinerate one cube and keep another (see the Companion Cube section).
